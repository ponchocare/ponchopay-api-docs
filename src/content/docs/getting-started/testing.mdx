Build and test against the demo environment (`https://demo.ponchopay.com`). No real money moves
there. To place test orders and receive webhooks you need a demo account: ask your account manager
or email [support@ponchopay.com](mailto:support@ponchopay.com).

Each payment method must be turned on in your demo admin before you can test it.

## Trigger webhooks

First set your webhook URLs in the demo admin under
[Settings → API integration](https://demo.ponchopay.com/provider/admin/settings). To reach a server
on your laptop, use a tunnelling tool such as ngrok or Cloudflare Tunnel.

| Webhook                                                                      | How to trigger it on demo                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [`payment_captured`](/webhooks/events/payment-captured/)                     | Pay by card, Pay by Bank or Express Tax-Free Childcare in checkout. Or, with payment guarantee turned on, enter card details for the guarantee on a Standard Tax-Free Childcare or voucher payment. |
| [`payment_reported_complete`](/webhooks/events/payment-reported-complete/)   | Pay with Standard Tax-Free Childcare or vouchers and report the payment as complete in checkout.                                                                   |
| [`payment_completed`](/webhooks/events/payment-completed/)                   | Sent automatically once the payment is complete. Card, Pay by Bank and Express Tax-Free Childcare payments: finish checkout. Standard Tax-Free Childcare and vouchers: report the payment as complete in checkout, then mark it in bank (below). |
| [`payment_in_bank`](/webhooks/events/payment-in-bank/)                       | In production this is sent when bank reconciliation runs. On demo, simulate it: on the [payments page](https://demo.ponchopay.com/provider/admin/payments), use **Mark in bank** at the end of the row. It's available for completed, reported-complete and late payments when reconciliation is turned on. Card payment methods can't be marked in bank. |
| [`payment_refunded`](/webhooks/events/payment-refunded/)                     | Pay by card, then [refund the payment method](/endpoints/refund-payment-method/) or refund it from the admin. |
| [`payment_cancelled`](/webhooks/events/payment-cancelled/)                   | Select a payment method in checkout without finishing, then [cancel the payment](/endpoints/cancel-payment/) or cancel it in the admin. No webhook is sent if no payment method was selected. |
| [`payment_updated`](/webhooks/events/payment-updated/)                       | [Update a payment method](/endpoints/update-payment-method/) on a Standard Tax-Free Childcare or voucher payment, or change it in the admin. |
| [`recurring_payment_captured`](/webhooks/events/recurring-payment-captured/) | [Initiate a subscription](/endpoints/initiate-subscription/) and complete the card pre-authorisation (for Tax-Free Childcare or vouchers, with payment guarantee turned on). |
| [`recurring_payment_set_up`](/webhooks/events/recurring-payment-set-up/)     | [Initiate a subscription](/endpoints/initiate-subscription/) and finish setting it up. |
| [`recurring_payment_cancel`](/webhooks/events/recurring-payment-cancel/)     | Set up a recurring payment, then [cancel it](/endpoints/cancel-subscription/) or cancel it in the admin. |

## Before you go live

Run at least these tests, and check both the PonchoPay admin and your own system each time:

- [ ] A card payment. Check every webhook arrives and is handled.
- [ ] A Tax-Free Childcare payment. Check every webhook arrives and is handled.
- [ ] A childcare voucher payment. Check every webhook arrives and is handled.
- [ ] A voucher payment where the parent enters their card but doesn't finish checkout.
      `payment_completed` must not be sent.
- [ ] A Tax-Free Childcare payment where the parent enters their card but doesn't finish
      checkout. `payment_completed` must not be sent.
- [ ] Every payment appears in the PonchoPay admin, and PonchoPay's transactional emails arrive.
- [ ] Every payment appears in your system, and your own actions (such as confirmation emails)
      happen.

<Aside title="Switching to production">
  Change the base URL to `https://pay.ponchopay.com/api/` (with the SDKs, `https://pay.ponchopay.com/`),
  use your production integration key, and set your webhook and redirect URLs in the production
  admin.
</Aside>
