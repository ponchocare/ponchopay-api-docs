The body of every payment webhook. It's sent as JSON with a `signature` header so you can check it came from PonchoPay: see [handling a webhook](/webhooks/events/payment-completed/#handle-the-webhook).

Headers: `signature` (HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body), `content-type: application/json` and `user-agent: PonchoPay/<version>`.
