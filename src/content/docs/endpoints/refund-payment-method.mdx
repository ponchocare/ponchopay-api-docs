<ApiColumns>
  Refunds all or part of one payment method within a payment. The money goes back to the card or
  account the parent paid with. You can refund in several parts, up to the payment method's amount.

  Only payments can be refunded. Recurring payments (subscriptions) generate payments: to give
  money back, refund the payment they generated, not the recurring payment.

  PonchoPay sends [`payment_refunded`](/webhooks/events/payment-refunded/) when the payment is refunded.

  <Aside type="caution" title="Card and card-charged payment methods only">
    Card payment methods (including Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna and Pay by Bank) can be refunded
    here, and so can Tax-Free Childcare or voucher payment methods that were charged to the parent's
    card through the payment guarantee. Otherwise, Tax-Free Childcare refunds must go back to the
    parent's Tax-Free Childcare account, and voucher refunds go through the voucher provider.
  </Aside>

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Endpoint method="PUT" path="/api/payment-method/{paymentMethodId}/refund" />
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>

## Authentication

<ApiColumns>
  Send a [management JWT](/getting-started/authentication/#management-jwt) in the `Authorization`
  header, with `sig` computed from the exact JSON body you send. The SDKs build the token for you.

  <Fragment slot="code">
    ```http
    PUT /api/payment-method/{paymentMethodId}/refund
    Authorization: Bearer <jwt>
    Content-Type: application/json
    ```
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>

## Returns

<ApiColumns>
  **`204 No Content`**: the refund was requested. Wait for
  [`payment_refunded`](/webhooks/events/payment-refunded/) to confirm it.

  **`400 Bad Request`** or **`409 Conflict`**: nothing was refunded. Common messages:

  | Status | Message | What to check |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | `400` | `The refund amount must be greater than 0.` | `amount` is `0` or less. |
  | `400` | `Requested refund amount exceeds the refundable amount.` | `amount` is more than what's left to refund. |
  | `400` | `A card payment was expected but it could not be found.` | The payment method has no card payment to refund. |
  | `400` | `Original card payment reference could not be located.` | The original card payment can't be found to refund against. |
  | `400` | `Unable to refund payment method.` | The payment method isn't a card payment method and hasn't been charged to the parent's card. |
  | `400` | `Something went wrong` | Among other causes, the payment method has already been fully refunded. |
  | `409` | `A refund is already in progress. Please wait for it to complete.` | Wait for the earlier refund's webhook, then try again. |

  An invalid or expired token is rejected before any of these checks.

  <Fragment slot="code">
    ```http title="Response"
    HTTP/1.1 204 No Content
    ```
