<ApiColumns>
  PonchoPay sends webhooks to your server as payments and recurring payments move on: when the
  parent leaves their card, when the payment completes, when the money reaches the provider's bank,
  and when anything changes. Implementing them correctly keeps your system in sync with PonchoPay,
  reducing the risk of discrepancies, missed payments or poor experiences for parents. If you don't
  follow the recommended implementation, your integration may be incomplete and you won't get the
  full value of PonchoPay.

  A payment has no payment method until the parent selects one in checkout, and PonchoPay sends no
  webhooks for it until then.

  Each event has its own URL. Set the ones you want in the PonchoPay admin under **Settings → API
  integration**. You can use the same URL for several events: the body's `event` field says which
  one it is.

## Payment lifecycle

<ApiColumns>
  Four events follow a payment from checkout to the provider's bank:

  1. [`payment_captured`](/webhooks/events/payment-captured/): the parent completed the card
     pre-authorisation for a Standard Tax-Free Childcare or voucher payment. Also sent at the same time as
     `payment_completed` for card, Pay by Bank and Express Tax-Free Childcare payments. For Standard
     Tax-Free Childcare and vouchers, not sent if payment guarantee is turned off.
  2. [`payment_reported_complete`](/webhooks/events/payment-reported-complete/): the parent says
     they've paid outside PonchoPay, with Standard Tax-Free Childcare or vouchers. It's their claim,
     backed by the card guarantee if payment guarantee is turned on.
  3. [`payment_completed`](/webhooks/events/payment-completed/): the payment is done. For Standard
     Tax-Free Childcare and vouchers this only comes once PonchoPay finds the money in the bank,
     which can be 2 or more days later because of voucher providers' payment terms.
  4. [`payment_in_bank`](/webhooks/events/payment-in-bank/): PonchoPay found the payment in the
     provider's bank account. Not available for every payment.

  Which ones you get, and when, depends on how the parent paid.

  <details>
  <summary>Legacy lifecycle (before 25 April 2025)</summary>

  Integrations set up before 25 April 2025 may still use the old lifecycle, which has no
  `payment_reported_complete`. In it, `payment_completed` is sent as soon as the parent reports a
  Standard Tax-Free Childcare or voucher payment as paid, not when the money is confirmed. The card
  pre-authorisation acts as a backup in this case, if payment guarantee is turned on. To move
  to the current lifecycle, email [support@ponchopay.com](mailto:support@ponchopay.com).

  </details>

  <Fragment slot="code">
    ```txt title="Card, Pay by Bank, Express Tax-Free Childcare"
    checkout finished      → payment_captured
                           → payment_completed
    money found in bank    → payment_in_bank
    ```

    ```txt title="Standard Tax-Free Childcare, childcare vouchers"
    card guarantee left    → payment_captured
    parent reports paid    → payment_reported_complete
    money found in bank    → payment_completed
                           → payment_in_bank
    ```
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>

## Changes to a payment

<ApiColumns>
  These events tell you when something about a payment changes. Not every payment can be changed.

  - [`payment_updated`](/webhooks/events/payment-updated/): the amount or a payment method changed,
    for example through [Update a payment method](/endpoints/update-payment-method/).
  - [`payment_refunded`](/webhooks/events/payment-refunded/): all or part of the payment was
    refunded.
  - [`payment_cancelled`](/webhooks/events/payment-cancelled/): the payment was cancelled.

  All payment events send a [PaymentCallback](/objects/payment-callback/) body.
</ApiColumns>

## Recurring payments

<ApiColumns>
  Recurring payments (subscriptions) have their own events, with a
  [SubscriptionCallback](/objects/subscription-callback/) body:

  - [`recurring_payment_captured`](/webhooks/events/recurring-payment-captured/): the parent
    completed the card pre-authorisation for a recurring payment. For card recurring payments it's
    sent just before `recurring_payment_set_up`; for Tax-Free Childcare and vouchers, not sent if
    payment guarantee is turned off.
  - [`recurring_payment_set_up`](/webhooks/events/recurring-payment-set-up/): the recurring payment
    is ready to take payments.
  - [`recurring_payment_cancel`](/webhooks/events/recurring-payment-cancel/): the recurring payment
    was cancelled.
</ApiColumns>

## Request format

<ApiColumns>
  Every webhook is a `POST` with a JSON body and these headers:

  <Fields schema="WebhookHeaders" />

  PonchoPay doesn't expect a response body: reply with any `2xx` status. An event can be manually
  resent from PonchoPay with the same `id`, so make your handler safe to run twice.

  <Fragment slot="code">
    ```http title="Example request"
    POST /webhooks/ponchopay HTTP/1.1
    Content-Type: application/json
    User-Agent: PonchoPay/<version>
    Signature: <base64url HMAC-SHA256 of the body>

    {"id":"…","date":"…","event":"payment_completed","payment":{…},"request":{…}}
    ```
