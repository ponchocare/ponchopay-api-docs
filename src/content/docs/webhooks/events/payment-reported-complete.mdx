<ApiColumns>
  Sent when the parent tells PonchoPay they've paid with Standard Tax-Free Childcare or childcare
  vouchers. They pay on the government's or the voucher provider's site, so PonchoPay can't see the
  payment yet.

  This is the parent's claim, not proof. The card they left as a guarantee backs it up. When
  PonchoPay finds the money in the provider's bank, you get
  [`payment_completed`](/webhooks/events/payment-completed/).

  Use it to show the payment as pending. Don't treat it as paid yet.

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Event name="payment_reported_complete" setting="Payment reported complete" />
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>

## The event

<ApiColumns>
  PonchoPay sends a `POST` with the [standard headers](/webhooks/overview/#request-format) and a
  [PaymentCallback](/objects/payment-callback/) body, with `event` set to `payment_reported_complete`:

  <Fields schema="PaymentCallback" />

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Example schema="PaymentCallback" title="Example body" overrides={{ event: 'payment_reported_complete', 'payment.method': 'tax_free_childcare', 'payment.payment_methods.0.type': 'tax_free_childcare', 'payment.payment_methods.0.variant': 'manual' }} />
    The values are examples. `request.metadata` is the `metadata` you sent when you created the
    payment.
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>
