<ApiColumns>
  Sent when PonchoPay finds the payment in the provider's bank account, as part of reconciliation.
  The money has been collected and is available to the provider.

  Until PonchoPay confirms it (with `payment_completed`, or with this event in the legacy
  lifecycle), a payment the parent reported as made outside PonchoPay might not actually have been
  made. Payments made with an express checkout method are guaranteed by now.

  Not every payment gets this event. Use it to mark the payment as reconciled or settled.

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Event name="payment_in_bank" setting="Payment in bank" />
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>

## Test it

<ApiColumns>
  On the demo environment, complete an Express Tax-Free Childcare payment (or report a Standard
  Tax-Free Childcare or voucher payment as complete), then use **Mark in bank** at the end of its row on the
  [payments page](https://demo.ponchopay.com/provider/admin/payments). It's available for completed,
  reported-complete and late payments when reconciliation is turned on. Card payment methods can't
  be marked in bank. In
  production this happens when bank reconciliation runs.

  See [Testing](/getting-started/testing/#trigger-webhooks) for how to set your webhook URL on demo.
