<ApiColumns>
  Sent when PonchoPay finds the payment in the provider's bank account, as part of reconciliation.
  The money has been collected and is available to the provider.

  Until this point, a payment the parent reported as made outside PonchoPay might not actually have
  been made. Payments made with an express checkout method are guaranteed by now.

  Not every payment gets this event. Use it to mark the payment as reconciled or settled.

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Event name="payment_in_bank" setting="Payment in bank" />
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>

## Test it

<ApiColumns>
  On the demo environment, complete a payment (or report a Standard Tax-Free Childcare or voucher
  payment as complete), then use **Mark in bank** at the end of its row on the
  [payments page](https://demo.ponchopay.com/provider/admin/payments). It's available for completed
  and reported-complete payments, and card payments, when reconciliation is turned on. In
  production this happens when bank reconciliation runs.

  See [Testing](/getting-started/testing/#trigger-webhooks) for how to set your webhook URL on demo.
