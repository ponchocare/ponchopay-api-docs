<ApiColumns>
  Sent when all or part of a payment is refunded, through
  [Refund a payment method](/endpoints/refund-payment-method/) or the admin. Not every payment can
  be refunded.

  - `payment.refunded_amount` is the amount of **this** refund.
  - `payment.refunded_total` is the total refunded so far.
  - In `payment_methods`, the same two fields show which method the refund came from.

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Event name="payment_refunded" setting="Payment refunded" />
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>

## The event

<ApiColumns>
  PonchoPay sends a `POST` with the [standard headers](/webhooks/overview/#request-format) and a
  [PaymentCallback](/objects/payment-callback/) body, with `event` set to `payment_refunded`:

  <Fields schema="PaymentCallback" />

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Example schema="PaymentCallback" title="Example body" overrides={{ event: 'payment_refunded', 'payment.refunded_amount': 1000, 'payment.refunded_total': 1000, 'payment.payment_methods.0.refunded_amount': 1000, 'payment.payment_methods.0.refunded_total': 1000 }} />
    The values are examples. `request.metadata` is the `metadata` you sent when you created the
    payment.
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>
