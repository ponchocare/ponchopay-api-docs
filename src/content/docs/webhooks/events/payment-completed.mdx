<ApiColumns>
  Sent when the parent has done everything needed for the payment to be taken. This is usually the
  event to act on: mark the order as paid, confirm the booking, or send your own receipt.

  - **Card, bank or Express Tax-Free Childcare payments**: as soon as checkout finishes and the funds
    are captured. For card and Express Tax-Free Childcare,
    [`payment_captured`](/webhooks/events/payment-captured/) is sent at the same time, unless
    payment guarantee is turned off.
  - **Offline payments, such as Standard Tax-Free Childcare or childcare vouchers**: once PonchoPay
    has confirmed the payment is in the provider's bank. This can be 2 or more days after checkout, depending on the scheme's
    payment terms.

  <Aside title="Parents paying outside PonchoPay">
    With Standard Tax-Free Childcare and childcare vouchers, the parent pays on the government's or
    voucher provider's site. When they tell PonchoPay they've paid, you get
    [`payment_reported_complete`](/webhooks/events/payment-reported-complete/). That's the parent's
    claim, backed by the card they left as a guarantee. `payment_completed` only follows once the
    money is confirmed.
  </Aside>
