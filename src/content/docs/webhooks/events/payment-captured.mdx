<ApiColumns>
  Sent when the payment details have been captured: the parent made a card payment, or completed the
  card pre-authorisation for a Tax-Free Childcare or voucher payment. From this point the payment can
  be guaranteed even if the parent abandons it midway, if the payment guarantee option is selected
  in your settings.

  - **Card or Express Tax-Free Childcare**: sent at the same time as
    [`payment_completed`](/webhooks/events/payment-completed/).
  - **Standard Tax-Free Childcare or childcare vouchers**: sent when the parent completes the card
    pre-authorisation, before they pay outside PonchoPay.

  Not sent if payment guarantee is turned off.

  Use it to hold the place or booking. Wait for `payment_completed` before treating the payment as
  paid.

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Event name="payment_captured" setting="Payment captured" />
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>
