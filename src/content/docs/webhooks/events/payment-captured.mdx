<ApiColumns>
  Sent when PonchoPay has the parent's card details. From this point the payment is guaranteed,
  even if the parent doesn't finish, as long as payment guarantee is turned on in your settings.

  - **Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, Pay by Bank or Express Tax-Free Childcare**: sent when
    checkout finishes, at the same time as [`payment_completed`](/webhooks/events/payment-completed/).
  - **Standard Tax-Free Childcare or childcare vouchers**: sent when the parent leaves their card as
    a guarantee, before they pay outside PonchoPay. Not sent if payment guarantee is turned off.

  Use it to hold the place or booking. Wait for `payment_completed` before treating the payment as
  paid.

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Event name="payment_captured" setting="Payment captured" />
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>
