<ApiColumns>
  Sent when the parent completes the card pre-authorisation for a Tax-Free Childcare or voucher
  recurring payment: its payment details have been captured. From this point the payments it
  generates can be guaranteed, if the payment guarantee option is selected in your settings.

  Not sent if payment guarantee is turned off.

    <Event name="recurring_payment_captured" setting="Recurring payment captured" />
  </Fragment>

<ApiColumns>
