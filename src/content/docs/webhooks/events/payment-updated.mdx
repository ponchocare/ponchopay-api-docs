<ApiColumns>
  Sent when the amount of a payment or one of its payment methods changes in PonchoPay, for
  example through [Update a payment method](/endpoints/update-payment-method/) or in the admin. Not
  every payment can be updated.

  The body has an extra `updates` field listing what changed, as JSON paths into the body:
  `$.payment.amount` if the amount changed, and `$.payment.payment_methods[n]` for the payment
  method that changed. Fields that change as a result, such as `payment.method`, aren't listed. Update your copy of the payment from the
  `payment` object. If a payment method's type changed, it has a new ID: the old payment method
  is cancelled and replaced.

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Event name="payment_updated" setting="Payment updated" />
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>
