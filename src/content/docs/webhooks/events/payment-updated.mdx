<ApiColumns>
  Sent when the amount of a payment or one of its payment methods changes in PonchoPay, for
  example through [Update a payment method](/endpoints/update-payment-method/) or in the admin. Not
  every payment can be updated.

  The body has an extra `updates` field listing what changed, as JSON paths into the body, such as
  `$.payment.amount` or `$.payment.payment_methods[0]`. Update your copy of the payment from the
  `payment` object.

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Event name="payment_updated" setting="Payment updated" />
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>

## The event

<ApiColumns>
  PonchoPay sends a `POST` with the [standard headers](/webhooks/overview/#request-format) and a
  [PaymentCallback](/objects/payment-callback/) body, with `event` set to `payment_updated`:

  <Fields schema="PaymentCallback" />

  <Fragment slot="code">
    <Example schema="PaymentCallback" title="Example body" overrides={{ event: 'payment_updated', updates: ['$.payment.payment_methods[0]'], 'payment.method': 'childcare_vouchers', 'payment.payment_methods.0.type': 'childcare_vouchers', 'payment.payment_methods.0.variant': 'edenred' }} />
    The values are examples. `request.metadata` is the `metadata` you sent when you created the
    payment.
  </Fragment>
</ApiColumns>
